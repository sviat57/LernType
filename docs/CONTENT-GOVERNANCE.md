# Content and assessment governance

## Publication gate

Every production item requires a stable semantic ID, CEFR level, skill, objective, task family, revision, accepted-answer policy, author, reviewer, license and provenance. Audio also requires transcript, speaker/license metadata and playback policy.

An exam pack is advertised as supported only when it covers every required task family, contains at least three unseen parallel mock forms and has a practice bank at least twice the size of one mock. Informational blueprints remain labelled as reference material until that gate is met.

## Review

- Two independent DaF/DaZ reviews for language, level, ambiguity and cultural appropriateness.
- Automated schema, duplicate-ID, asset, checksum, answer-key and timing validation.
- Receptive-item reliability target α/ω ≥ 0.80 after a representative pilot.
- Productive-score agreement target weighted κ/ICC ≥ 0.75.
- Critical content defects below 0.5% of published items.

AI may assist drafting but no AI-only item is published. Generated comprehension questions from a private book remain personal practice and do not contribute high-confidence exam evidence.

## Official formats

Official names are descriptive references. LernType does not use provider logos, copy protected tasks or imply affiliation. Source URLs, retrieved date, scoring policy and blueprint version are stored per exam and reviewed at least every six months.
